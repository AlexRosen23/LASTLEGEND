# Last Legend — Post V10 / V11+ delivery plan

Status as of 2026-10-23. Features under review are not considered published without green CI and deployment.

## Done before V11
- V6 human/nonhuman stylized animation and separate Identity Lab
- V7 spectator director, character-follow controls, top-10 boards, local champion history
- V8 real website routes: Home, Play, Legends, Champions, How to Play, About, Updates
- V9 zone escape priorities and deterministic regression suite
- V10 500 scenario definitions with event/arena/body gating and optional content intensity

## V11.0 — Character Dossiers and Readability (current)
- Two independent visible actions: SHOW STATS and ADD TO DRAFT on both Legends and Draft cards
- Clicking a character portrait/card opens a complete game dossier; full 20-attribute bars, 5 behavioral tendencies, role/team/edition, signature and all secondary abilities
- Portrait, profile provenance and clear fictional-game disclaimer; historical editions remain separate competitors
- Once drafted, selected slots open the dossier; a separate Remove control removes a character
- Restore legibility for dense/bold headings and mobile text
- Desktop/390px e2e for all controls and no horizontal overflow; 2–100 match engine and V10 parity unchanged

## V11.1 — Full Big Four Roster Expansion (not implemented yet)
- Import date-stamped current NHL, NFL, NBA, MLB rosters using authorized, attributable, license-reviewed sources
- Current active-roster scope; canonical ID, team, sport, status, retrieval timestamp, source URL, edition; handle injured/two-way/call-up, trades, diacritics and duplicates
- Preserve 1,203 historical/crossover originals and seven additional eras; keep catalogs searchable and paginated instead of dumping thousands of DOM cards
- Verify rights to real athlete likenesses, team marks and marketing use before broad launch

## Proposed subsequent phases
- V12 — True individualized encounter choreography: per-body/costume/emote animation rigs for the 500 V10 scenarios, arena props, cinematics, reduced-motion alternatives
- V13 — Smarter opponents & arenas: obstacle-aware path planning when real physical blockers exist; strategic terrain, resource decisions, opponent anticipation, balance audits for every creature and athlete archetype
- V14 — Public performance, accessibility and trust: virtualized huge catalogs, fast load and touch interactions, keyboard/focus/contrast audits, SEO/social preview, content moderation and imagery/trademark review
- V15 — Persistent competition, only with a real backend: shareable seeded challenges, competitions, authentic leaderboards and opt-in cross-device accounts. Needs secure storage, moderation, privacy, abuse prevention and rate limiting
- V16+ — Expanded spectator presentation and statistical analytics: matchup builder, probability distributions from *repeated deterministic simulations* with methodology stated, athlete-level fictional performance histories and deeper replay tooling

Nothing about online accounts, global leaderboards, full Big Four active rosters, personalized encounter motion rigs or multiplayer is implied to be live in V11.
